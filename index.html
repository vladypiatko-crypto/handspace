Gestures
  • ☝️ Point (index finger straight, others folded) to draw in the air.
  • 🤏 Pinch something to grab it and move it. Twist your wrist to turn it.
  • Pinch it with both hands to scale and rotate it.
  • 🤏🤏 Pinch both hands in empty space and pull apart to mark a white drawing area (or another shape from the shape picker). Point at the area to draw on it.
  • ✋ Hold your open palm up for a second to open the menu around your hand. Pinch an item to choose it. Pinching any button on screen presses it, so you never need to click.
  • Hold a fist ✊ to undo. Hold ✌️ for the next shape.
  • 🍎 Gravity: let go while moving to throw. Slime squishes and splats.
  • 📌 Stick: draw on your face, hand or a recognized object (cup, phone, bottle…), or drop something onto it, and it sticks and moves with it. Grab it to pull it off.
  • 😋 Eat: bring something to your mouth, open wide, then close. Three bites and it's gone.
  • Mouse/touch: drag to draw, drag an object to move it, Shift-drag or right-drag for a shape, Alt-drag to draw on an object, scroll for depth.
