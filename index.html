Gestures
  • Point with your index finger (others folded) to draw in the air.
  • Pinch something to grab and move it. Twist your wrist to turn it. Pinch it with both hands to scale and rotate.
  • Pinch both hands in empty space and pull apart to make a drawing area (or the shape picked in the toolbar). Point at it to draw on it.
  • Or frame it: make an L with thumb and index on both hands, like framing a photo, and hold still for a moment.
  • Hold an open palm up for a second to open the menu around your hand, then pinch an item. Pinching any button on screen presses it.
  • Hold a fist to undo. Hold a peace sign for the next shape.
  • Gravity: let go while moving to throw. Slime squishes and splats.
  • Stick: draw on or drop things onto your face, hand or a recognized object, and they move with it. Grab to pull off.
  • Eat: bring something to your mouth, open wide, then close. Three bites.
  • Mouse: drag to draw, drag an object to move it, Shift-drag for a shape, Alt-drag to draw on objects, scroll for depth.
