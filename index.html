Gestures
  • Pinch (thumb + index) — draw, grab, paint or erase depending on the tool.
  • Draw on objects — start a stroke on a shape and it sticks to its surface and moves with it.
  • Pinch with both hands and pull apart — in Shape mode, stretches a new shape between your hands.
  • Grab with both hands — scale and rotate an object.
  • Hold a fist for a moment — undo.
  • Hold a peace sign ✌️ for a moment — switch to the next tool.
  • Move your hand closer to or further from the camera to draw in depth.
